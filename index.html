FAQ

Operational guide

Daily Workflow

What should staff do each day?

Start on Dashboard, open Review Queue, approve only proven required fields, then use Contracts or Reports when someone needs an answer.

What does Needs Review mean?

The app found a possible value but does not have enough proof. Open the source, verify the wording, correct it if needed, then save.

What is the Review Queue?

It is the worklist for contracts that are not ready yet. Each row should be opened, checked against the source file, saved, and submitted when complete.

When is a contract ready to submit?

Required fields must be saved and source-proven or manually PDF verified. Optional fields should not block approval.

Source Proof and OCR

What if OCR is wrong?

Open Review Queue, choose the field, use Source/Search or PDF preview, correct the value, then Save Field. The correction helps future matching.

Should the app guess missing values?

No. If the source does not prove vendor, facility, date, cost, renewal, or termination, the app should leave it blank or mark Needs Review.

Can I search inside the OCR text?

Yes. Use Source/Search in review or the OCR/PDF compare view. Search for words like payment, invoice, term, renew, terminate, notice, fee, rate, or service.

Can Word files be reviewed?

Yes. PDF, Word, image, pasted text, and ShareSync files can be uploaded. The original file should remain linked so staff can open the real source.

Required Fields

What fields are required?

Contract type, vendor name, facility, effective date, cost/fee/rate, auto-renewal, and how to terminate or notice period.

What if there is no fixed end date?

Use auto-renews / no fixed end date when the contract continues until terminated. Do not create an end date unless the contract states one.

Where does facility belong?

Facility is its own required field. Vendor and facility should not be combined. If the contract has a legal entity name, use aliases to connect it to the facility.

Where do per-mile or per-service fees go?

Save the actual rate and unit, such as $2.00 per mile or $25 per square foot. Annualized spend should calculate only when the app has enough quantity/frequency proof.

ShareSync and Files

Where do contract files live?

ShareSync remains the file cabinet. The app keeps searchable data and links back to the real PDF or Word file.

When does a file save to ShareSync?

After review/approval, uploaded source files should file to the correct facility folder. If the facility is unknown, choose it before approval.

Can the app delete ShareSync files?

No. Deleting in the app removes the app record only. Actual ShareSync files must be deleted directly from ShareSync/File Explorer.

How do I check the ShareSync connection?

Go to Upload or Admin. The ShareSync status should show read access, save access, and folder visibility.

Search, Finance, and Reports

How do I find a contract?

Use Contracts. Search by vendor, facility, service, contract name, fee, payment term, renewal language, notice, or OCR words.

Why does finance say missing cost?

A contract may have a rate but not enough frequency or quantity to calculate annual spend. Save the fee, unit, billing cycle, and quantity when the contract proves it.

What is PPD?

PPD is annualized contract spend divided by facility beds and 365 days. It only works when the contract has usable cost and the facility has a bed count.

Can I make my own report?

Use Reports or Finance filters, choose columns, then export. Saved custom report templates should be used for repeat leadership reports.

Users, Learning, and Admin

How does learning work?

Corrections teach vendor aliases, facility aliases, service categories, source wording, and field patterns. Learning should make review faster, not auto-approve unsupported data.

How do user roles work?

Admin creates users, assigns role and scope, and can later limit users by facility, team, or field responsibility.

What is scope?

Scope is what a user is allowed to see or work on, such as all facilities, selected facilities, finance work, vendor cleanup, or assigned review fields.

What is Active Staff / Current Work?

Admin can see who is active, their current page, the contract or review item they opened, and when they were last seen.
